I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the **[HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/Vicent0205)**, part of the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, supervised by **Professor Junxian He**. I received my **B.Eng.** degree from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024 (2020-2024), where I was also previously advised by Professor Junxian He, and received the *Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship*.

My research focuses on **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** and **Machine Learning (ML)**. My specific research interests include:
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
